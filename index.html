Core Capabilities

Built for speed, security, and absolute autonomy.

Screen Vision

ARIA can "see" what is on your screen. It processes UI elements and visual data contextually without taking screenshots to the cloud.

Cross-App Overlay

Whether you're in a browser, a code editor, or a game, ARIA overlays seamlessly. Triggered by Alt+Space or text selection.

Agentic Workflow (Router)

The system intelligently routes your queries. Simple grammar checks use fast local LLMs, while complex code refactoring spins up dedicated isolated agent instances. A master orchestrator in your OS.

Deeply Integrated.

ARIA doesn't just talk to you. It talks to your apps. Connect your favorite services and let ARIA control them locally.

Plugins are lazy-loaded only when used, ensuring absolute zero bloat on your system memory.

The Engine Room

The relentless evolution from v2.0 to v2.5

v2.0 & v2.1: Foundation & Vision

The core architecture, fluid UI, native Voice Engine, and Screen Vision were born. ARIA learned to see and speak.

v2.2: Local AI Shield

The end of cloud dependency. LocalTextRouter enabled zero-cost translation, grammar fixing, and summarization entirely on local GPUs.

v2.3: Smart FAB

Context-aware Floating Action Buttons brought AI directly to the user's cursor across the entire OS.

v2.4: Safe Swarm

Hierarchical multi-agent delegation with strict Human-in-the-Loop safeguards for catastrophic commands.

v2.5: Night Shift

Long-term autonomy. Hand ARIA a massive task before bed, and wake up to a perfectly orchestrated report. Fully implemented and operational.
